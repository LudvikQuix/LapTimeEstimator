# Open Points: lap-simulation-csv-driver

## OP-1 — Rerun viz: blueprint failure mode is silent (2026-05-22)

The `build_blueprint()` function in `viz/scene3d.py` wraps the entire `rrb.*` construction in `except Exception: return None`. When it fails, the viewer auto-layouts with no user-visible error. **Decision needed:** should the exception be re-raised (crashing loudly), logged to stderr with a warning, or turned into a degraded-but-documented state? Current silent fallback hides the root cause of the missing chase-cam pane entirely.

## OP-2 — Rerun viz: ghost-vs-sim speed unit alignment (2026-05-22)

The telemetry panels log `speed_ms` and `speed_kmh` for both traces separately. When the auto-layout places them in different panels, ghost/speed_ms and sim/speed_kmh are side-by-side but in different units — confusing. **Decision needed:** should the speed comparison use a single unit (km/h) with both ghost and sim on the same panel, or keep them as separate panels and add a unit suffix to the panel name/entity path? Rerun 0.32 `TimeSeriesView` does not support shared multi-trace panels from separate entity paths in the blueprint API (needs verification).

## OP-3 — Rerun viz: `contents=["/**"]` in Spatial2DView — compatibility with Rerun 0.32.2 (2026-05-22)

The chase-cam view uses `rrb.Spatial2DView(origin=chase_cam_path, contents=["/**"])`. The `/**` glob is intended to pull ALL world geometry into the camera reprojection. It is unknown whether this syntax works in Rerun 0.32.2 or silently produces an empty view / raises and triggers the `except Exception` fallback. **Decision needed:** ArchDev should test `rrb.Spatial2DView` with `contents` specified vs unspecified to determine if this is the root cause of the missing third pane.

## OP-6 — Phase 5.0.9 spec needed: terminal v-tracking cost from DP envelope (2026-05-24)

Phase 5.0.8 falsified the hypothesis that emit-path changes alone could close the MPC chicane abort (`docs/architecture-slip-model-phase5_0_8-v32-first-class-longitudinal.md`). The diagnosed root cause is brake-anticipation: at 70 m/s the QP's 30 m horizon (0.43 s lookahead) cannot see the chicane braking zone 170+ m ahead, so `w_v · (v_x − v_ref)²` does not commit a brake until far too late. Two possible fixes were identified; one is rejected (raising horizon 5× pushes solve time past the 20 ms tick budget). The remaining candidate is a terminal v-tracking cost driven by the DP plan envelope: add `J_term_v = w_term_v · (v_x[N] − v_envelope[N])²` where `v_envelope[N] = min(v_ref(s_N : s_N + 150 m))` — a value-function approximation at the horizon's tail that says "don't be faster than the long-range future minimum or you've committed to a brake you can't deliver." Standard MPC terminal-set technique; no geometric horizon increase needed. **Decision needed:** Buddy to spec Phase 5.0.9 — `MPCWeights.w_term_v`, `LongitudinalPlan` envelope lookup, single new cost term in `mpc_qp.build_qp`. Alternative: declare MPC parked and confine to steering-only (current `--mpc-emit-source sub` path with reactive as the production controller), which is what `docs/architecture-v3-shipping-state.md` already documents.

## OP-7 — Phase 5.2 HMPC inner-tracker friction-circle headroom (2026-05-24)

Phase 5.2 (`docs/architecture-v3-hmpc-casadi-outer.md`) closed the outer-planner infeasibility gap by replacing OSQP+SQP with CasADi+IPOPT. Outer primal-infeasibility now 0/11 solves (was 80 % under OSQP+SQP). Brake-first-> 0.5 commit moved from s ≈ 391 m → s ≈ 299 m (vs reactive baseline s ≈ 257 m). Closed-loop completion still 0/10 — abort at s ≈ 631-647 m. Diagnostic: the outer plans `a_long ≈ −8.6 m/s²` (max friction-circle decel) on the lead-in brake zone, but the closed-loop chassis decelerates at only ~6.3 m/s² (73 % of friction limit). The remaining 25 % friction headroom is the gap between the outer's plan and the chassis's realised brake. Mathematically, with the unused headroom recovered, chassis would arrive at the chicane apex at v ≈ 13 m/s (matching DP plan) rather than v ≈ 40 m/s (current).

The Phase 5.2 brief explicitly excluded changes to `hmpc_inner.py` / `mpc_qp.py`. Three candidate root causes for the inner under-committing brake:

1. **Inner `w_v` weight too low.** The inner cost on `(v_x − v_ref_outer)²` is 10.0; at typical Δv = 5 m/s the cost contribution per stage is 250, comparable to (but not dominating) `w_lat · n² + w_slip · slip²`. Sweep candidate: bump `w_v` to 25-50 and re-measure.

2. **Inner friction-ellipse safety margin reserves grip.** `mpc_qp_ellipse.py` enforces an ellipse on `(F_long, F_lat)` per axle inside the inner QP. The ellipse exponent and safety fraction may be conservative; check whether `--hmpc-force-static-fz` (which disables dynamic Fz redistribution) changes the brake-commit shape.

3. **Inner actuator rate limits.** `MPCBounds.brake_dot_max = 5.0 / s`; at a 50 Hz tick that's 0.10 brake / tick. From 0 to 0.95 brake takes 9.5 ticks (190 ms) — meaningful at 70 m/s (13 m of travel). Sweep candidate: bump to 10.0 / s.

**Decision needed:** Buddy to scope Phase 5.3 — a focused inner-tracker tuning pass driven by the Phase 5.2 outer that is known to produce the right reference. Or, alternatively, the v3.6 acados migration (the brief mentioned the outer's solve time would drop from ~300 ms to ~5-10 ms, enabling 5-10 Hz outer cadence which would tighten the loop and reduce the inner's load).
